Operations overview

Cyber Watchdog

A hosted watch over the live Sentinel APEX feed: evidence-cited threat priority, customer relevance and an analyst triage inbox. It classifies items already on the feed. It does not scan networks, watch the dark web or invent advisories when the feed is stale.

Sign in

Pro Defense, Enterprise SOC or MSSP

Your API key is exchanged once for a short-lived, Watchdog-only session (15 minutes, refreshed while this tab is open, at most 4 hours). The key is not stored in this browser, not put in the URL, and the field is cleared after sign-in. Your watches keep running when the tab closes: the hosted scheduler evaluates them after each feed update.

Delivery

Integrations

Signed HTTPS webhooks to your SIEM or SOAR. A destination receives nothing until it passes verification: we POST a signed challenge and your endpoint must answer {"challenge": "<nonce>"}. Every delivery is signed X-CDB-Watchdog-Signature: v1=hex(HMAC-SHA256(secret, timestamp + "." + raw_body)). HTTPS on port 443 only; private, loopback, link-local and metadata addresses are refused at registration and before every delivery. Redirects are not followed.
