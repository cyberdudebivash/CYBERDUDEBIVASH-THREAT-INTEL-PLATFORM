Webhook destinations

Enterprise SOC and MSSP. A destination receives nothing until it passes verification: we POST a signed challenge and your endpoint must answer {"challenge": "<nonce>"}. Every delivery is signed: X-CDB-Watchdog-Signature: v1=hex(HMAC-SHA256(secret, timestamp + "." + raw_body)). HTTPS on port 443 only; hosts that resolve to private, loopback, link-local or metadata addresses are refused at registration and before every delivery. Redirects are not followed.
