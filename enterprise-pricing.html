FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers here — full detail on the pricing FAQ and billing & API FAQ.

Is there a free trial?

Community is free forever (50 API calls/day, no card required). PRO, Enterprise, and MSSP are billed the full listed price immediately at checkout — that payment is your first month, and access is provisioned automatically.

What payment methods are accepted?

Razorpay (UPI, Cards, Net Banking, EMI) or Gumroad (Cards, PayPal, Stripe). GST invoices are issued automatically for every paid plan.

How fast do I get my API key?

Instantly for the vast majority of payments — provisioned and emailed automatically the moment Razorpay or Gumroad confirms payment.

What SLA do paid plans include?

Enterprise and MSSP plans include a 99.9% uptime SLA, with critical alerts pushed via Telegram within 15 minutes of detection.

Can my MSSP resell this intelligence?

Yes — the MSSP white-label program lets you brand the dashboard and API under your own name. See the MSSP program for partnership tiers.
