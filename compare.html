Switching from another platform? Common questions
"We already use Recorded Future. Why add SENTINEL APEX?"
SENTINEL APEX is not a replacement for Recorded Future's analyst-led strategic intelligence. It is a self-serve operational feed: IOCs, detection rules and STIX 2.1 at a published price. Some teams use both, a strategic platform plus a lower-cost operational feed.
"CrowdStrike already includes intelligence with our endpoint platform."
SENTINEL APEX is vendor-neutral: it delivers STIX 2.1 over TAXII 2.1 and SIEM feeds for Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel and QRadar, and its Sigma rules work regardless of your EDR. It can sit alongside your endpoint platform.
"We self-host MISP, and it is free."
MISP is free and powerful; you also run the servers, curate feeds and maintain integrations. SENTINEL APEX is a managed feed you can pull into MISP over TAXII 2.1, so many teams use both.
"Do you have SOC 2 Type II?"
Our Trust Center describes our SOC 2 Type II readiness and the controls behind it. Enterprise customers can request security documentation through the briefing page.
"Can we bring intelligence from another platform?"
SENTINEL APEX publishes intelligence over TAXII 2.1; it does not import other vendors' data. Most teams run SENTINEL APEX alongside their existing platform during evaluation and compare results.
"We are in India. Can you issue a GST invoice?"
Yes. SENTINEL APEX is registered under Indian GST (GSTIN 21ARKPN8270G1ZP). We issue GST-compliant tax invoices in INR, with checkout via Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking). Indian customers pay listed INR prices plus 18% GST; see the pricing page for ₹ amounts.
