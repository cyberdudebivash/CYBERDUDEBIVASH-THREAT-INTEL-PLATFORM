Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need to pay to get PRO, Enterprise, or MSSP access?+
Yes. There is no free trial; the FREE tier is available without payment. PRO, Enterprise and MSSP are billed immediately at checkout via Razorpay or Gumroad — there is no separate, smaller "trial" charge and no card-verification hold. There is no automated recurring billing; you decide when to renew, with no surprise charges.
How long does access take after checkout?+
Your API key is provisioned automatically once Razorpay or Gumroad confirms payment — no manual review step. Support operates IST business hours with best-effort off-hours coverage.
How do I cancel or stop being billed?+
There is no automated recurring billing, so there's no subscription to cancel to avoid a future charge. If you'd like to stop using a paid tier, email billing@cyberdudebivash.com and simply don't renew when your access period ends. See our Terms for the full refund policy.
Can I upgrade from PRO to Enterprise later?+
Yes. Email enterprise@cyberdudebivash.com and we'll help you move to Enterprise; the price difference is prorated against your current period.
Does Enterprise/MSSP include a SIEM integration call?+
Yes. Enterprise and MSSP access includes a live onboarding call (2 hr and 3 hr respectively) where we connect your SIEM and push your first detection rules. PRO access includes async documentation, video guides, and Slack community support.
Is there a free trial for PRO, Enterprise, or MSSP?+
No. There is no free trial on any plan; the FREE tier lets you evaluate the live feed and API without payment. For Enterprise or MSSP, book an evaluation call instead of checking out directly. Once you do check out, PRO, Enterprise, and MSSP charge the full listed price immediately; that payment is your first month, not a separate "trial" or "proof-of-intent" charge.
Can my team share one account?+
Yes. A single API key can be shared across your SOC team members. Enterprise supports up to 5 named users with individual audit logs. MSSP can provision 5 sub-tenant environments for client deployments.
What payment methods are supported?+
We accept automated, instant checkout via Razorpay (UPI — GPay / PhonePe / Paytm; Cards; Net Banking) or Gumroad (Cards, PayPal). Indian customers pay + 18% GST. For invoicing requirements, email billing@cyberdudebivash.com.
